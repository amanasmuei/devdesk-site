FAQ

Questions, answered

The things everyone asks before their first request.

How much does it cost? +

It depends on the job — which is exactly why we quote first. You get a fixed price before any work starts, and it never changes mid-project.

When do I pay? +

After you've seen the work functioning — a live preview or screen recording. You confirm it does what you asked, then pay via DuitNow / TnG. No deposits on small jobs.

What if I don't like the quote? +

Then you walk away — completely free. Requesting a quote costs nothing and commits you to nothing.

Do we need to meet or call? +

Never. Everything happens in writing. Clearer requirements, better records, zero awkward calls.

What if it breaks after delivery? +

Every job includes one free revision round after you test. If something we built stops working as delivered, we fix it.
